# Lineage and sources

`claude-stfu` combines the author's own Claude Code preferences and case-study claim rules with ideas from:

- Anthropic's [output-style guidance](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/output-styles)
- the move catalog in [`slop-no-more` v0.2.1](https://github.com/calebstacy/slop-no-more/tree/v0.2.1)
- John Swales' 1990 [*Genre Analysis*](https://shop.cambridge.org/english/product/2700033225)
- Ken Hyland's 2005 [*Metadiscourse*](https://www.bloomsbury.com/us/metadiscourse-9780826476104/)

The author's preferences and case-study rules are unpublished inputs. The public sources informed the vocabulary and structure; none of them validates this prompt's behavioral effect.
